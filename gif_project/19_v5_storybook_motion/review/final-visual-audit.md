# V19 final visual audit

**Verdict: no new major compositing or style defect found in the final scene samples.** The warm illustrated atmosphere has been restored. The remaining concern is the limited number of distinct motion poses, rather than broken ground or cutout artifacts.

Reviewed the final `exports/poster.png`, `review/entry-exit-review.jpg`, decoded MP4 sample sheet, current Evan v2 scene/contact sheets, Mir full-pose source and timing contact. These correspond to the 23.04-second loop, 45.5 px/s travel, Mir B timing, and Mir body y249±1.

- **Ground:** the grassy edge, narrow golden path and dark soil/rock cross-section read as one painted platform. No new floating strip, pasted seam or duplicated ground band is visible. Evan's soles meet the path instead of hanging below it. The prominent soil layer is part of the original scenery and fits the intended side-view fantasy setting.
- **Tree and sky:** the sampled wind phases retain coherent branches and leaf edges. The exposed clouds, house roof and door frame do not show an obvious bend, tear or moving join. The heavy trunk and foreground remain visually anchored.
- **Characters:** Evan's purple/gold clothing and cyan staff, and Mir's friendly purple body, pale wings and golden horns, fit the scene. No visible red fringe or black alpha edge appears. The staff does not penetrate the ground. Mir reads as deliberately flying low, with clearance under the downward wing; it does not look accidentally detached from a walking surface.
- **Entry/exit:** characters enter and leave at the screen edges in their travel direction. The contact sheet shows a short empty-background interval around the offscreen wrap, with no visible teleport or duplicate pair.
- **Style:** the result returns to the warm storybook illustration requested after rejecting literal game sprites. Character outlines remain a little cleaner than the textured scenery, but they do not look like imported game assets or unrelated stickers at the delivered 800×348 size.

**One specific visual limitation remains:** Mir's 2↔3 wing-angle change is large. The B order removes the original 4→5 recovery jump but cannot supply the missing intermediate angle. This is a motion-sampling limitation, not a reason to deform the finished illustration. The combined player review is the appropriate final check for whether that step and Evan's gait cadence are acceptable; no additional static-scene correction is recommended from this audit.

Only this review note was written during the final audit; renderers, source art and exports were not modified.
